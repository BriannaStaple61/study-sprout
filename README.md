# 🌱 Study Sprout
**Grow your plant. Grow your progress.**

## About the Project
Study Sprout is a cozy assignment tracker designed to help students like me who struggle with procrastination. It uses a virtual plant companion to make completing assignments feel more motivating and rewarding.

## Problem Statement
I struggle to start responsibilities early when they are not due immediately. I often put things off until they become urgent, which causes me to rush and sometimes affects my school, work, and personal life.

This happens because procrastination has become a habit. Even when I plan and prioritize my responsibilities, I sometimes delay them until the last minute.

I'll know this problem is improving when I can complete my responsibilities without delaying them so long that they interfere with other responsibilities.

## Existing Solutions
- **Phone calendar:** I use reminders, but sometimes dismiss them and tell myself I'll do the task later.
- **Blackboard:** I check assignment deadlines, but the countdown sometimes only motivates me when the deadline is close.
- **Browser tabs:** I leave unfinished assignments open, but this doesn't consistently motivate me to finish them.

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
Study Sprout currently allows users to:

1. Name their virtual plant companion.
2. Add assignments with a name, class, and due date.
3. Mark assignments as completed.
4. See the plant's happiness meter increase as assignments are completed.
5. Save assignments in the browser so they remain after refreshing.

## Technology Used
- **HTML:** Website structure
- **CSS:** Styling, colors, plant design, and animations
- **JavaScript:** Assignment tracking, plant happiness, and interactions
- **Local Storage:** Saves assignments in the browser
- **GitHub:** Stores project code and development history

## How to Use
1. Open `index.html` in a web browser.
2. Name your plant companion.
3. Continue to the assignment tracker.
4. Enter an assignment name, class, and due date.
5. Click **Add Assignment**.
6. Check off completed assignments to increase your plant's happiness.
